Why a timetable

Seasonal collections are departures: each has a date, a time, a destination and remarks. The app's main job is to say what leaves next and what needs your sign-off before it does. The direction lends four things and nothing else: type, palette, density and one signature move. Layout, navigation and controls stay the standard ones you already know.

Palette
Four inks. Paper white (deep ink in dark mode), black ink, timetable yellow only for the next change and for selection, signal red only for failures and the today hand.
Type
Archivo, one family, with tabular figures. Narrow widths for dates and column heads, normal width for reading.
Signature
The upcoming-changes timetable (Date · Change · Collection · Library · Via · Remarks), with the next departure posted on yellow.
Ruled out
Holiday decoration and terminal or log-viewer styling, per your answers. Kometa's seasonal posters appear only as content.

What's real and what's illustrative

Preset names, windows, sources, list sizes and example titles come from Kometa v2.5.3 and live IMDb and MDBList queries on 2 Nov 2026. Owned counts, library names, Radarr and Sonarr options and versions come from the 2 Nov server spikes. Review counts and service states are still illustrative. Posters are placeholders apart from Kometa's seasonal images.
