Missing: design notes (Phase 2)

What you own and what you're missing, per collection, with sending to Radarr or Sonarr one step away. Titles and IDs are real TMDb and IMDb entries; owned counts are illustrative.

  1. 1

    Movies and Shows tabs

    Two tabs with counts, as the brief asks. For TV, "missing" means series missing from a list, universe or franchise. Missing episodes stay Sonarr's job. The add cap is shown here as well, since this screen spends it.

  2. 2

    Monitor the TMDb collection

    For TMDb franchise collections, a callout offers to hand the whole collection to Radarr (/api/v3/collection). Radarr then adds members as they are released. The copy says that plainly, because Radarr skips announced and unreleased members.

  3. 3

    Send panel, not a modal

    The options sit in a side panel next to the selection, with every dropdown filled live from Radarr: quality profile, root folder with free space, minimum availability, tags, monitored and search on add.

    The guardrail checks and the cap are shown before you commit. "Send to Review instead" queues the add with these options. Excluded and already-present titles are disabled in the list, with the reason given.
